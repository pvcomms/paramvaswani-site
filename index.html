principles
  1. Dissect the package. What is said, how it’s sold, what actually arrives — three different things.
  2. Keep the exit. Any system you cannot leave owns you.
  3. Judgment is the last thing to outsource. Automate the work; never the choice of which work is worth doing.
  4. Question the frameworks you question with. Every instrument has assumptions baked in — especially the ones that feel like plain glass.
  5. Impact over intent — in both directions, including when I’m the one who meant well.
  6. Credences, not vibes. Numbers keep me honest about what I actually believe. See the ledger.

I overcorrected with the machine — every hour planned, every note processed, every decision routed through it. Then cold turkey. Both were positions the pendulum picked, not me. The right relationship keeps shifting; that’s probably the honest answer.
aug 2026wiring
The vocabulary preceded the clarity. Getting language for how my nervous system actually runs wasn’t pathologising — it was finally being able to describe myself to myself. A neurodivergent mind in a neurotypical world is a translation job, and I finally have the dictionary.
aug 2026power / tech
The algorithm gave me language, community, intellectual access. It also flattened things, extracted attention, made ambient dissociation feel normal. Genetics plus environment — and the environment was built by a business model nobody consented to.
