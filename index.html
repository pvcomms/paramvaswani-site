Raised by the algorithm, realigning by hand.

principles
  1. Dissect the package. What is said, how it’s sold, what actually arrives — three different things.
  2. Keep the exit. Any system you cannot leave owns you.
  3. Judgment is the last thing to outsource. Automate the work; never the choice of which work is worth doing.
  4. Question the frameworks you question with. Every instrument has assumptions baked in — especially the ones that feel like plain glass.
  5. Impact over intent — in both directions, including when I’m the one who meant well.
  6. Credences, not vibes. Numbers keep me honest about what I actually believe. See the ledger.
