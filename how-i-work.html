How I Work

revised30 sep 2026

the setting

I usually pick an Airbnb / Serviced Apartment in a residential suburb of a big city (Copenhagen last month, Berlin this month).

I look for a kitchen + balcony + work setup + fast wifi (which I check online before) + ability to receive packages (mostly cables / specific shampoo type of stuff so my personal care / body / mind systems can endure regardless of geography & I can travel light).

I unpack and “make myself at home immediately” + identify a cafe + walk path (and build familiarity with the route).

the structure

With my mental illnesses & just horrible to manage ADHD, I struggle with severe executive dysfunction & intellectualized rumination so I try to create systems + stakes + consequences as well as clear and defined purpose + goals + long-term coherence.

I’ve found work-life balance level of compartmentalization insanely difficult and instead use outcome KPIs + external “real-person” audits to prevent subject-object bias & yield objective evaluation.

Instead, I operate in bursts of days to weeks long bursts of goal oriented obsession and alternate work / play swathes of time centered around a clearly defined purpose + KPI (that indexes for my compulsion towards perfectionism by letting me accept that “I’m done” when I’m 80 percent satisfied).

I tend to spend a lot of my attention & focus on overthinking, feeling non-realistic and non-embodied anxiety, low self-worth & self-hatred compulsive loops that manifest as Recursive guilt & shame. And as much as I am able to step-away and gauge the inherent irrationality and non utility of such thought patterns, I find this to be my biggest obstacle.

So, I’ve had to create systems that make it near impossible to view these as “mere thoughts,” as “brain tricks,” and as “UnTruths.”

Highly meticulous self-created structure is the solution I’ve found + extensive intentional “Digital Environment Engineering”.

To my brain, the illusion of control or ostensible control FEELS the SAME as control, so here I try to hold the “truth fax” / “implications” / “self-deception” as loosely as possible and gauge system tweaks / “is it working” on outcome / actual “threading the needle” KPI review every 10 days.

work phase system

|cycle| = 10 days

pre-work

  1. Loop Earplugs + ANC Sonos Ace or Dyson
  2. 15 minutes of Sun + replies w a custom news podcast + a bit of movement
  3. Warm lighting + “seeing the sun” or some semblance of “the sun seeing me” + mini white noises machine cooking
  4. AM 100mg caffeine + 100mg L-Theanine + Vit B12 + Vit D
  5. Unload + Brush + Wash night goo off face
  6. Vegan Yogurt + Blueberries + Granola with Lonely Island Playlist & “Dopa” Site Loop (The Guardian + Vox + The Economist + HackerNews + BBC + Aeon + Psyche + The Verge + Marginal Revolution + TechCrunch) - 15 minute timer
  7. Wash, Dry & Restock Yogurt Dish (“Circular Method” attempt)
  8. Head to “work”

while working

  1. I keep next to me: Green Tea Unsweetened + Sparkling Water 200/500ml + Still Water 1000ml + electrolyte sachet
  2. A3 Size “Art Book” + iPad Pro 11 inch with Apple Pencil Pro + MacBook Pro M4 + wireless keyboard (I go thru em; Logitech “multi connect” preferred but using apple one rn) + Logitech MX Master 3S
  3. Mac Studio M3 Ultra 96GB 1TB + 1TB SSD + Yubi NFC Passkey (+ spare) on my wrist + 2 Thunderbolt Cables + 1 HDMI Cable
  4. TV Screen (if I’m lucky, my apartment / room will have one) else connected to iPad Pro or I buy a monitor if I end up cribbing

snack for blood sugar / when “peckish” or hangry

“lentil salad” / “hummus” / sourdough crackers / plum / nectarine / hazelnuts / walnuts

anxiety breaking

5 minute meditation / hype song / 50 push ups / rationalization of perceived threat to establish safety

post-work

  1. Walk around the block + custom podcast recapping my day’s inputs + “placing” them in my “knowledge base / memory palace” - 15 min
  2. Wash & chop & lightly sauté to al dente veggie variety (broccoli, cauliflower, asparagus, bell peppers (colors!), squash, zucchini, etc.) + half an avocado AND half a heirloom tomato, usually over arugula + rocket + baby spinach w Vampire Weekend / HAIM / Bon Iver / Sharon Van Etten Concert
  3. To a “relax chair” (never same one I inhabit during “work”) for media consumption: TED Talk / Big Think Video / Less Wrong Reading / Substack Reading / Audiobook w Kombucha (or lately it’s been effervescent prebiotic + probiotic tabs)
  4. 2x weekly: MUBI / Apple TV Store Purchased Film (that I decide in advance) w Kombucha (or lately it’s been effervescent prebiotic + probiotic tabs)

head to bed

  1. Magnesium
  2. Conan / DarkNet Diaries / “light” podcast with Therabody Legs & Therabody Goggles

9 hours minimum (non negotiable).

REPEAT.

1 day off every 10 days of cycle as a “Recovery Day” for groceries + laundry + deep clean + logistics planning + TV watch.

  3. Add a run / workout 3x weekly & actually adhere
