ontology.tree — what exists, and how firmly
  1. ?What is there, and where do the cuts fall?
    1. 1Minds exist, and can be read from the inside.
      1. 1aFirst-person experience is evidence — one subject, error bars left in.
      2. 1bNeurodivergence is a difference in wiring, not a deficit.
        1. iAttention has a sign, and the environment sets it.
        2. iiAnxiety is readiness misread, not a disorder.
    2. 2Judgment is a faculty of its own, distinct from computation.
      1. 2aTaste exists: distance from the mean, chosen on purpose.
      2. 2bCognition splits into logistics and judgment; only the first is delegable.
      3. 2cThe delegation discount is real: the artifact arrives, the comprehension doesn't.
    3. 3Mediation is constitutive, not neutral.
      1. 3aEvery instrument carries assumptions, especially the ones that feel transparent.
      2. 3bExternalisation runs on a gradient: storage, then retrieval, then generation.
      3. 3cThe personalisation loop closes on the faculty it feeds from.
    4. 4Systems divide by whether you can leave them.
      1. 4aAny system you cannot leave owns you.
      2. 4bPower and the tools to leave it concentrate in the same place.
    5. 5Beliefs are credences: numbers that commit you to update.
      1. 5aA credence is a commitment to update, not a position to defend.
      2. 5bA held contradiction is an object, not a bug.
    6. 6Some things are cut out on purpose.
      1. 6aIntent is not a currency.
      2. 6bThere is no neutral pipe.
      3. 6cThe mean is a location, not a verdict.
